Wigho AI — Product Requirements Document
Tagline: Never lose the conversation or commitment behind your business.

1. Overview
Wigho AI is an AI-powered business communication and productivity platform built for small business owners, founders, and freelancers. It unifies a user's business conversations (Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs) into a private, AI-powered memory layer, then proactively acts on that memory — surfacing follow-ups, generating tasks, managing calendar commitments, and drafting replies — so nothing a user has promised a client or partner ever falls through the cracks.
2. Problem Statement
Small business owners and freelancers run every function of their business themselves — sales, support, admin — across scattered channels (email, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs). The biggest cost isn't a lack of tools; it's forgetting what was promised to whom. Existing tools (CRMs, task apps, inboxes) store information but don't actively remember or act on commitments on the user's behalf.
3. Target User
Small business owners
Solo founders and freelancers
Anyone running their business primarily through direct conversations rather than a dedicated sales/support team
4. Product Pillars
Personal Memory — private, user-level memory of the individual's own history and preferences.
Business Memory — memory built from connected business channels: Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram.
Authorized AI Action — the AI only acts on data and channels the user has explicitly connected and authorized.
Private by Default — single-user memory model at launch; no forced team/multiplayer complexity.
5. Core Features
5.1 Unified Business Memory
Ingests and understands conversations from Gmail, WhatsApp, and Instagram DMs.
Extracts commitments, deadlines, and open threads automatically from natural conversation.
Two distinct memory layers: personal and business, with the AI acting only within what the user is authorized to access.
5.2 Proactive Actions (triggered by deadlines/commitments only)
Follow-up nudges — reminds the user of commitments as deadlines approach or pass (e.g., "You told Sarah Friday — it's Thursday.").
Smart search — pull-based search across all connected conversations and memory.
Auto-draft replies — context-aware draft responses based on conversation history.
Auto-generated tasks — tasks created automatically from conversations, always linked back to the conversation and client that created them.
Proactivity is strictly deadline/commitment-triggered — not real-time or high-frequency — to avoid notification fatigue and stay tightly scoped to the core promise.
5.3 Productivity Modules (bundled, conversation-linked)
Tasks/to-dos — born from and linked back to the originating conversation.
Calendar & scheduling — commitments and deadlines surface directly on the calendar.
Invoicing & payments — tied to client conversations and commitments.
Client/contact CRM records — a living record built from actual conversation history, not manual data entry.
5.4 Memory & Access Model
Single-user, private by default at launch.
No team-sharing or multiplayer permissions complexity in v1 — reserved for a future phase once the single-user experience is validated.

7. Onboarding Flow
Welcome (0:20) — "Welcome to Wigho AI: your business conversations, commitments, and follow-ups, understood and organized."
Guided demo (1:00) — a 60-second guided demo showing how Wigho understands and organizes commitments.
Connect your business — user connects their first channel (Gmail) and sees real, immediate value (e.g., "Found 3 open commitments you haven't followed up on.").
Add another channel — WhatsApp and Instagram added only after initial value has been demonstrated.
Business brief & daily handoff — onboarding closes into the daily experience: "Good morning — here's what needs your attention."
8. Platform
Mobile app first — primary experience, since WhatsApp/Instagram are mobile-native and deadline nudges need to reach users wherever they are.
Web app as companion — for deeper review, setup, and configuration.
9. Pricing Model
Multiple tiers, structured around channel count and memory/action depth:
Tier
Suggested Scope
Basic
Single channel (Gmail), core memory + follow-up nudges
Pro
All channels (Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram) + full action suite (tasks, calendar, drafts, CRM)
Enterprise
Pro features + future team-sharing/multiplayer memory

10. Success Metrics (suggested starting points)
% of new users who connect at least one channel within first session
% of users who see a surfaced commitment/follow-up within first 5 minutes
Weekly active usage of proactive nudges (opened/acted on vs. ignored)
Retention at 30/60/90 days
Upgrade rate from Basic → Pro
11. Out of Scope (v1)
Team/shared memory and multi-user permissions
Real-time/high-frequency notifications
Additional channels beyond Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram
Desktop-first experience

This PRD reflects product decisions made collaboratively; feature scope, pricing, and onboarding details should be revisited as user research and early usage data come in.
